Approved transfer entries

Record a TE so the return shows the corrected position. A TE either corrects this month's cash account (it goes into Table-1's Monthly Cash Account) or rectifies a difference carried from an earlier month (it goes into Table-2's "Rectified During the Current Month") — never both.

Discrepancy register

The CBS Daily Discrepancy Reconciliation Register (Annexure-IV Table-3). Serial numbers restart each financial year; the register is preserved permanently and checked at inspection.

Settle entry
